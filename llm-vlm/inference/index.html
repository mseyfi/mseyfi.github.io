Where quantization helps: weight-only vs weight–activationsurvey §5.2.1

  • Decode is GEMV and memory-bound: weight-only (W4A16, W8A16) helps
  • Prefill is GEMM and compute-bound: you need W8A8 or FP8 to use faster tensor cores
  • Survey test (A100, AWQ W4A16): decode about 2.0–2.4× faster at batch 1 and 1.2–1.4× at batch 16; prefill about 0.8–1.1×, often slower
  • The gain shrinks as batch and context grow; bigger models gain more
